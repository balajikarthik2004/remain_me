# 👑 Solo Leveling Desktop Companions — Real Beru & Igris

## Overview

A professional, high-definition Windows desktop companion application featuring **Solo Leveling's greatest Shadow Soldiers**: **Shadow Marshal Beru** and **Blood-Red Commander Igris**.

Both characters are rendered with **high-definition manhwa-grade artwork**, a **real-time living Shadow Monarch aura particle system**, **dynamic flying physics**, expressive **emotional states**, and **deep motivational dialogue** addressing you as their **King & Sovereign**.

---

## 🌟 Visual & Engine Features

### 1. 🔮 Real Shadow Aura Particle System
- **Volumetric Shadow Smoke**: Continuous wisps of dark purple/violet smoke swirling, expanding, and rising from their bodies.
- **Soulfire Embers**: Glowing electric cyan (Beru) and blood-crimson (Igris) energy embers drifting upward in real-time.
- **Shadow Domain Awakening**: When Awake Mode (`Ctrl+B`) is enabled, particle emission and aura intensity surge into a raging shadow tempest!

### 2. 🦅 Dynamic Flying & Floating Physics
- **Multi-Harmonic Hover**: Smooth floating hover combining multiple sine wave frequencies for natural buoyancy.
- **Directional Banking Tilt**: When flying across your desktop toward your cursor or patrolling, the character tilts into the flight path with trailing shadow mist.

### 3. 🎭 Expressive Emotional States
- **Hover / Idle**: Floating gracefully with living shadow smoke and drifting soulfire embers.
- **Crying (Tears of Devotion)**: When praised or petted, waterfalls of joyful anime tears pour down with floating hearts and stars (*"MY KING HAS TOUCHED ME!! 😭👑"*).
- **Royal Fealty (Bow)**: Homage to the Sovereign beneath a floating golden Monarch crown.
- **Cheer**: Triumphant leap with floating Monarch sparkle gems (*"YOU ARE THE BEST, MY KING!! 👑"*).
- **Flight**: Dashing across the screen to your cursor.

## Controls & Shortcuts

| Action | Control / Shortcut |
|---|---|
| **Toggle Shadow Domain (Awake)** | `Ctrl + B` (or right-click menu) |
| **Summon / Hide Companion** | `Ctrl + Shift + Q` |
| **Praise / Motivate** | Left-Click (triggers tears of devotion & victory cheer) |
| **Call to Cursor** | Double-Click |
| **Move Companion** | Click & Drag anywhere on screen |
| **Switch Character** | Right-Click ➔ `👤 Switch Character` ➔ `Beru` / `Igris` |
| **Change Size** | Right-Click ➔ `Companion Size` ➔ Small (110px) / Tiny (86px) / Medium (132px) |
